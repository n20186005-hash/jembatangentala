Jembatan Gentala Arasy, Jambi

Understand this Batanghari River pedestrian bridge, then check access for your visit. The Gentala Arasy tower and museum have separate visiting arrangements.

Location & orientation

The Google Maps point read on 19 October 2026 is -1.5866286, 103.6157117, with the listed address CJ78+87W, Jl. Raden Pamuk, Kasang, Jambi Timur, Kota Jambi 36123. A map point does not establish an entrance or vehicle drop-off. The Vice President’s inauguration report describes a link between Jambi City and Jambi Kota Seberang, leading to Menara Gentala Arasy. Agree the approach side with your driver and check road crossings on arrival.

Hours, fees & visit-day access

Google Maps on 19 October 2026 listed Monday–Saturday 07:00–00:00 (the following midnight), and Sunday 24 hours. This is a third-party listing snapshot, not a guarantee of continuous bridge access or safety after dark. Check access and closures with staff or signs before crossing. On the same date, Google Maps listed no wheelchair-accessible entrance. This is not an audit of every route; ask for specific access information before travelling. We have not verified working ramps, lifts, toilets, parking, seats or assistance services. Check these separately. Stay on permitted paths, do not climb railings, and avoid obstructing others. Change plans if rain, slippery surfaces, crowds or staff instructions make the crossing unsuitable.

A pedestrian bridge inaugurated in 2015

The Vice President’s official report of the 28 March 2015 inauguration gives the pedestrian bridge as 503 metres long and 4.5 metres wide, citing the governor’s report at the time. These are project figures from a 2015 source, not a new survey of today’s facilities.

Tower / museum: a separate visit

The Ministry of Culture’s museum registry describes Museum Menara Gentala Arasy as a specialist museum on the development of Islam in Jambi, managed by the provincial culture and tourism department. Its profile, read on 19 October 2026, lists Monday–Friday 07:30–16:00 and Saturday–Sunday 07:30–13:00, with Rp3,000; the public-holiday row is marked closed. This museum-profile reference is not a bridge price or schedule, nor a guarantee of ticket categories, upper-tower entry or lift operation. Recheck hours, your visitor category and accessible areas with the museum before visiting.

FAQ

Where is Jembatan Gentala Arasy?

The bridge crosses the Batanghari River in Jambi City. The point -1.5866286, 103.6157117 is a location reference, not a confirmed entrance; check your approach side before travelling.

Is the bridge open 24 hours?

This guide gives no such guarantee. Google Maps on 19 October 2026 listed Monday–Saturday 07:00–00:00 and Sunday 24 hours; check actual access and closures on site. The listing does not establish safety after dark.

What is the bridge entrance fee?

A current bridge fee has not been verified. Ask staff and read signs; the price on the Museum Menara Gentala Arasy profile does not automatically apply to the bridge.

Does the museum follow bridge hours?

No. The museum portal lists a separate schedule of Monday–Friday 07:30–16:00 and Saturday–Sunday 07:30–13:00, with public holidays marked closed, read on 19 October 2026. Recheck with its operator.

Is wheelchair access available?

Google Maps on 19 October 2026 listed no wheelchair-accessible entrance. Ask the operator to confirm routes and assistance; this page does not promise step-free access.

Is this the official operator website?

No. This is an independent guide without government or operator affiliation. A website link on Google Maps does not establish official status. We do not provide bookings, ticket sales or on-site visitor services.
