Analytics preferences use local storage; a tab refusal uses sessionStorage. If storage fails, the status explains whether refusal applies to this tab or only this page. We do not claim a permanent choice was saved when saving fails. Clear site data in browser settings to remove old permission. Analytics choices from older site versions are not automatically enabled in this version.
